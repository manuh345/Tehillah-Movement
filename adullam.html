The Inspiration

The Cave of Adullam

1 Sam 22

In 1 Samuel 22, David fled to the Cave of Adullam. There, the distressed, the indebted, and the discontented gathered to him — and he became their captain. God took broken people in a cave and transformed them into David's Mighty Men.

Adullam is that place for this generation. Every Saturday night, we open a sanctuary for those who are weary, spiritually hungry, and desperate for transformation. We believe that in the atmosphere of worship, prayer, and God's presence — God turns broken people into mighty vessels.

"Everyone who was in distress, everyone who was in debt, and everyone who was discontented gathered to him; and he became their commander." 1 Samuel 22:2
