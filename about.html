The Name

What is Tehillah?

Tehillah (תְּהִלָּה) is a Hebrew word meaning "praise" or "song of praise" — specifically the kind of praise that dwells in, and gives rise to, the manifest presence of God.

It is derived from the same root as Psalm 22:3 — "God inhabits the praises of His people." It is praise that hosts His presence. This is the heart of everything we do.

"But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel." Psalm 22:3
Purpose

Vision & Mission

VISION

The Dream

To raise a generation consumed by God's presence, living lives of worship, holiness, and revival — becoming vessels through whom God reveals His glory to the nations.

MISSION

The Work

Tehillah Movement exists to create atmospheres for God's presence through continuous worship, prayer, discipleship, and revival gatherings — preparing people to become yielded vessels that God can use for His glory.

What We Stand On

Core Values

01
The Presence of God

Everything we do flows from one conviction: God's manifest presence changes everything. We build atmospheres for Him to dwell.

02
Unceasing Worship

Inspired by the Davidic order, we believe in the power of continuous, unceasing worship and prayer before the throne of God.

03
Holiness & Consecration

We pursue a life of purity and consecration — vessels set apart for the Master's use, walking in spirit and in truth.

04
Global Revival

The fire of God is not for one city or nation. We are called to spread revival across the nations, igniting hearts everywhere.

05
Raising Vessels

We invest in developing Spirit-filled worshippers, intercessors, and ministers who carry God's glory into every sphere of life.

06
Unity of the Body

Revival comes when the Body of Christ unites. We gather voices from every stream and tribe around the throne of God.
