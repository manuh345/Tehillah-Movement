The Vision

365 Days of
Unceasing Worship

Revival Release is a 365-day, 24/7 worship movement dedicated to establishing unceasing worship and intercession before the throne of God — believing that sustained worship releases sustained revival across cities and nations.

Inspired by the Davidic model and the heavenly pattern of continuous worship in Revelation 4, we are raising a company of worshippers who will not stop until the knowledge of God's glory covers the earth.
